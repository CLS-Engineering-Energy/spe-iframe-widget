Purpose of the SPE Research & Development Technical Section Entrepreneurship Program Playbook v3

This version reframes the original startup workbook as a practical playbook for energy founders who need to bridge technical feasibility and commercial viability.

The energy industry is full of people with strong technical ideas. Engineers, scientists, geoscientists, and operators routinely identify inefficiencies, risks, and missed opportunities that could become useful products or services. The goal of this playbook is to help promising ideas move beyond a conversation or slide deck.

It is written for technical specialists, side-hustle innovators, academic pioneers, and industry veterans working across traditional infrastructure, climate-tech and decarbonization, and the digital layer of energy systems.

!

Reality check: energy startups face long sales cycles, pilot requirements, proprietary data, safety and reliability constraints, and extensive due diligence. These barriers are real, but once a solution is proven and embedded into operations, they can become a structural moat.

Guiding Principles

  • Problems matter more than solutions. A clear, expensive, recurring problem is the foundation.
  • Radical focus beats broad application. Narrow entry points improve learning, credibility, and traction.
  • Pilots must have commercial intent. Success criteria and next steps need to be explicit.
  • Community is a strategic asset. SPE and RDTS connections can accelerate feedback, mentorship, and adoption while founders still protect confidential IP.
